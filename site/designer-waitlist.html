How the Create-a-Thon works

Get your materials

Donated clothing, fabric and trims from the Fashion Lab stash, plus whatever thrift finds you bring.

48 hours in the lab

Two days of designing, cutting and sewing alongside the other designers, with machines, irons and food on hand.

Walk it down the runway

Your look is worn by a model or a Sensational Sedonan in the Fashion Lab Sedona show.
