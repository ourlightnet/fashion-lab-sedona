Leah Joy Love

Born into a multi-generational Arizona family that has been in the Verde Valley for almost 200 years, Leah began shooting at nine in the darkroom with her dad's vintage 35mm. She captures moments of transformation and growth on the red rocks, and photographed the Fashion Lab Sedona 2026 runway at Brady Hall.
