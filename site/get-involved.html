Help make the next Fashion Lab

Fashion Lab Sedona runs on donated fabric, borrowed machines and a lot of generous people. Here are all the ways to be one of them.

or write to info@fashionlabsedona.com

Ways to give

Every gift, big or small, goes into the next show. Pick a way below and we'll write back with the details.

01

Give money

Cash gifts pay for venues, fabric and the show. Fashion Lab Sedona is a project of LightNet, a 501(c)(3), so gifts may be tax-deductible.

Give money
02

Give crypto

We accept cryptocurrency donations. Email us and we'll send wallet details.

Give crypto
03

Clothes and fabric

Thrift finds, vintage, fabric and trims. Your closet clean-out becomes next year's runway.

Donate clothes
04

Sewing machines and gear

Sewing machines, sergers, irons, ironing boards, dress forms and notions for the Create-a-Thon.

Donate gear
05

Storage space

A garage, shed or spare room in the Sedona area to hold donated clothes and gear between events.

Offer space
06

Donate a raffle item

Gift cards, goods or experiences for the show raffle. Raffle donors are thanked on our Sponsors page.

Donate a raffle item
07

Lead a workshop

Teach sewing, upcycling, dyeing, styling or modeling at a pop-up or workshop.

Offer a workshop
08

Volunteer

Backstage, front of house, setup, pop-ups and workshops, on show weekend and all year.

Five ways to help

Pick one. Or all five.

01 · Donate materials

Donate clothing, machines and fabric

Clothing, sewing machines, ironing boards and material. Your closet clean-out becomes next year's runway.

Offer a donation
02 · Volunteer

Volunteer for the show and all year

Backstage, front of house, setup, pop-ups and workshops. There's a job for every kind of helper, on show weekend and throughout the year.

Volunteer
03 · Design

Become a designer for 2027

Create a collection from thrift finds and reclaimed fabric in 48 hours, then see it walk the runway. Registration for 2027 opens in the spring.

Join the designer waitlist
04 · Give

Donate resources or finance

Funds, venues, food, printing, services. Fashion Lab Sedona is a project of LightNet, a 501(c)(3), so gifts may be tax-deductible.

Talk to us about giving
