Event Collaboration

Fashion Lab Sedona is proud to collaborate with Sedona Dance Academy, a dance studio in Sedona founded in 2020 and an immersive community fostering friendship, artistry, athleticism and soul.

Denise Barr, Mary Dancer Browne and Jesselynn Desmond helped sew the girls' outfits, while the dearly loved choreographer Danielle Johnson of Sedona Dance Project organized the girls from Sedona Dance Academy to perform at the show. The girls got a how-to-model workshop from Nathan Quinn.

What is Fashion Lab

Re-imagine what you already own

The fashion industry produces about 92 million tons of textile waste a year, and less than 20% of textiles are reused or recycled. Fast fashion's short cycles and overproduction make it worse every season.

Fashion Lab Sedona empowers creators to re-imagine up-cycled garments and certified recycled fabrics into new designs. We support small-batch, local production that reflects who we are and brings the community together, one stitch at a time.

  • Upcycled & recycled only
  • Local designers

How it works

Three parts, one season

Monthly pop-ups

Free and low-cost workshops all season: sewing 101, bleach and natural dye, hip-pack making, hair flair, how-to-model classes, a movie night and a clothing swap. They grow the community and the volunteer base.

48 Hour Create-a-Thon

Designers register for $100 and spend one weekend building 1–2 looks from upcycled clothing and reclaimed fabric. Beginners welcome. Designs are turned in Sunday for judging.

The Fashion Show

One week later the looks walk the runway next to twelve "Sensational Sedonans", local personalities dressed by local boutique, The Closet Concierge. Vendors, VIP reception, raffle, awards, after party.

The Sustainable Fashion Design Award

$4,000 in courses and mentorship from the FABRIC Incubator in Tempe, plus a $1,000 cash award from Fashion Lab Sedona. A Team Player award recognizes the designer who lifted everyone else.
