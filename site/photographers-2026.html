Photographer · The Show

Leah Joy Love Photography

Fine art and editorial photographer, born into a multi-generational Arizona family that has been in the Verde Valley for almost 200 years.

Leah began shooting at nine years old, in the darkroom, with a vintage 35mm from her dad. Before photography became her full-time work she was a hotel marketing manager and a yoga festival director, and her 15 years of experience span yoga festivals and branding campaigns, music events, yoga teaching and hike guiding.

She holds space for people's stories and captures moments of transformation and growth on the red rocks, offering photo experiences, styling, movement and guided on-the-land experiences as a northern Arizona native. She calls herself a desert mermaid, and spends most winters sailing in New Zealand.

At Fashion Lab Sedona 2026 Leah photographed the runway at Brady Hall.

Photographer · Create-a-Thon and The Show

Karl Mullings, KMF Designs Photography

A professional photographer who has spent 25 years in northern Arizona capturing people at their most real: seniors, athletes, families, weddings and headshots.

Karl's path to the camera started with a box of flash cubes from his mom. It ran through 15 years in the U.S. Marines, a stretch when he quit photography entirely, and the mentors who pushed him back into his craft, plus an unexpected call from TLC to shoot Sister Wives. His newest project photographs 31 women in 31 days, honoring the mother who believed in him before he believed in himself.

Karl shot the 48 Hour Create-a-Thon and the runway for Fashion Lab Sedona 2026. Hear his story on the Awaken Your Purpose Podcast from the Holomovement Purpose Lab, hosted by Zenka Caro.
