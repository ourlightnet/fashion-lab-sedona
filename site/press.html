About Fashion Lab Sedona

In a paragraph

Fashion Lab Sedona is a community sustainable fashion event. Each fall, local designers spend a 48-hour Create-a-Thon turning thrift-store finds, discarded clothing and reclaimed fabric into new looks, which walk the runway a week later alongside Sensational Sedonans dressed by local boutique, The Closet Concierge. One designer wins a scholarship from FABRIC Incubator. Fashion Lab Sedona is a project of LightNet, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and the Dream Machine, bringing communities together in joy.
