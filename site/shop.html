2026 Headline Sponsor

The Closet Concierge

Sedona's high-end women's consignment and concierge closet boutique, owned by Betsy Klein.

For Fashion Lab Sedona 2026, The Closet Concierge ran the VIP room at the show and dressed all of our Sensational Sedonans for the runway. Stop in for designer clothing, luxury handbags, jewelry and shoes, personal styling, and closet clear-outs.

2026 Headline Sponsor

FABRIC Incubator

Arizona's fashion incubator in Tempe, lending professional resources and industry expertise to designers with sustainable visions.

FABRIC helps designers develop clothing lines through courses, mentorship and access to equipment for small-batch manufacturing. For Fashion Lab Sedona 2026, FABRIC donated $4,000 in courses and mentorship to the winner of the Sustainable Fashion Design Award.

FABRIC c/o NOVO Broadway, 711 W Broadway Rd
Tempe, AZ 85282
(602) 481-1032
