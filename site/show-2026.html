Sedona Dance Academy

Event Collaboration

Fashion Lab Sedona is proud to collaborate with Sedona Dance Academy, a dance studio in Sedona founded in 2020 and an immersive community fostering friendship, artistry, athleticism and soul.

Its teachers bring professional stage careers and training from schools such as the Kirov Academy, the Alvin Ailey School and Ballet Arizona, with classes in ballet, pointe, jazz, modern, Latin fusion and tumbling for dancers of all ages.
