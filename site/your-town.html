Why your town

Your town already has everything it needs

A thrift store. A few people who sew. A hall with a long aisle. Twelve people everybody knows. That is a Fashion Lab.

Fashion Lab Sedona started in 2024 with a core team of volunteers and a borrowed studio. By 2026 it was a sold-out show with twenty designers, sponsors, vendors, a raffle, a $5,000 design award and an after party. It builds a real creative community and keeps clothes out of the landfill.
